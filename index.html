01 THE CHALLENGE

Road concerns deserve
more than a message thread.

When concerns arrive through different channels, it becomes harder to organize evidence, identify the correct location and follow each report through to review.

02 THE RESEARCH

Built for a real local challenge.

The proposed framework supports DILG-aligned road-clearing and public-order monitoring in Santa Cruz, Laguna — connecting community participation with AI-assisted analysis and spatial intelligence.

⌖
STUDY LOCALESanta Cruz, Laguna, PhilippinesMunicipality-wide · 26 barangays
GENERAL OBJECTIVE◎

To design, develop and evaluate an integrated community violation reporting and monitoring system using machine learning-based multimodal classification and GIS spatial analysis.

SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES
  1. 01

    Collect and process crowdsourced reports with text, photos, coordinates, timestamps and selected concern types.

  2. 02

    Classify road-clearing and public-order concerns using NLP, supervised learning, object detection and multimodal processing.

  3. 03

    Integrate mobile and web reporting, GIS barangay matching, verification, status tracking and analytics.

  4. 04

    Evaluate functionality and software quality with technical experts and intended users using applicable ISO/IEC 25010:2023 criteria.

04 WHAT IT IDENTIFIES

The right category.
A better starting point.

CIVICLEAR classifies six text-report categories. Its image detector focuses on five visually identifiable violation classes.

A model output is a recommendation for review, not a final violation determination.
